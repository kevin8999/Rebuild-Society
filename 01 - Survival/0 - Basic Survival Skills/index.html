    Basic Survival Skills

    The first step to rebuilding society is to survive. If you do not survive, you have no chance of rebuilding society.

    Needs

    Resources

    In this repository, there is a book titled FM 21-76 US ARMY SURVIVAL MANUAL1. This manual contains all the information you'll need in a survival scenario. It even takes into account differing climates (such as deserts) and differing scenarios (such as nuclear fallout).

    Note that the survival manual is more focused on survival from a military context. However, many aspects of it are applicable to your situation as well.

    Another useful book is Search and Rescue Training SURVIVAL TRAINING.

    Chapter Pages

    Measurements

    Some parts of the book also mention the metric measurement system. Please see Metric System for more information. It will teach you how to derive the basic units. You will only need length initially, however.
